# Task 3: Data Visualization — Executive Business Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive visual analytics suite delivering business intelligence for an international retail enterprise. The project translates raw transactional data into structured, decision-ready visual formats including KPI scorecards, regional contribution charts, sub-category profitability matrices, and discount-margin sensitivity heatmaps.

## Visualizations Built
1. **Executive KPI Scorecard:** Displays Gross Revenue, Net Profit, Profit Margin %, and Average Order Value (AOV).
2. **Geographical & Fulfillment Breakdown:** Regional Sales vs. Profit parity bar charts alongside fulfillment tier cost spreads.
3. **Sub-Category Profitability Matrix:** Multi-dimensional bubble chart mapping Sales vs. Profit scaled by order frequency to isolate loss leaders from revenue engines.
4. **Temporal Trajectory:** Multi-series line plot tracking monthly revenue trends across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
5. **Discount Elasticity Heatmap:** Matrix diagnosing profit margin compression under escalating discount tiers.

## Core Business Findings
* **Discount Vulnerability:** Discounts exceeding 20% consistently trigger negative profit margins in Furniture and Office Supplies.
* **Top Revenue Anchor:** Technology sub-categories (Copiers, Phones) maintain the highest profit margin resilience.
* **Logistics Efficiency:** Standard Class provides predictable cost distribution, while Same Day shipping adds significant cost variance with minimal sales volume lift.
